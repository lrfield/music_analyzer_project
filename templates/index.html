Welcome!

This is a term project for Applied Database Technologies at Indiana University. It is a flask website that uses PyMongo to query and visualize the statistics of a MongoDB Database constructed from surveying listener and artist data from the Listenbrainz and Musicbrainz APIs.

The current source of the artist and listener data is from surveying the listeners of "seed artists" retrieved under the metal genre tag. So you can think of this data as representative of a sample of metal listeners, not a sample of metal artists.

Genres, artists, and listeners are hyperlinked whenever possible, so you can navigate to the analysis of any data item by clicking on it's hyperlink. For a general overview of the data, click the artist, genre, or listener tabs. For more information, navigate to the report tab. \m/...(`n`)…\m/.
